## Abstract
This article presents a technical service aimed at correcting the shape and grading of the waistband of fitness pants produced by a company whose original pattern exhibited mathematical errors and technical incompatibilities. The proposal involves identifying and analyzing inconsistencies in the provided pattern, followed by the application of concepts from analytic geometry and vectors to make the necessary corrections. The waistband, being an essential component in both the structure and aesthetics of the pants, directly influences the fit, comfort, and acceptance of the final product. Flaws in its construction can lead to issues such as inaccurate fitting, rework, and material waste, ultimately affecting the company's productivity. The methodology is based on a geometric analysis of the faulty pattern and the development of a new, technically sound version, while respecting the constraints imposed by the production process. At the end of the process, a corrected waistband is obtained—more reliable and better suited to market demands—without requiring comparison with the previous model. The proposal highlights the importance of a mathematical approach as a support tool for pattern making and for improving efficiency in the garment industry.
