## Abstract
The purpose of this article is to help identify Industry 4.0 (I4.0) main tools and trends applied in the textile sector in a sustainable manner, with the aim of fostering a discussion on a relevant research topic. The textile industry is one of the world’s largest sources of economic growth and industrial pollution, at a time when sustainability issues are becoming an increasingly pressing concern. As such, I4.0 offers technological alternatives designed to positively impact the apparel industry. A literature review was conducted using the systematic review method. The findings identified three main trends and their impacts on sustainability in the textile industry. Among these are: (1) the circular economy and sustainable production; (2) blockchain and the supply chain; and (3) Industry 4.0 tools applied to the textile industry. However, it is concluded that this article aims to serve as a tool to facilitate access to and raise awareness of potential implementations of sustainable innovations in the textile industry in Brazil.
