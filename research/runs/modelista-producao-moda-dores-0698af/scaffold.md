## Modality Classification Rationale

The user asks for an in-depth mapping of (1) the responsibilities and concrete tasks of a patternmaker (*modelista*) across the apparel/fashion production process, and (2) their pain points (*dores*) across every stage of that process. While this has an enumerative dimension (mapping phases and tasks), a high-value research report must also synthesize structural bottlenecks, technical vs. organizational tensions (e.g., styling vs. patternmaking, 2D CAD vs. 3D virtual prototyping vs. manual paper patternmaking, grading/measurement table standardization failures, fit approval loops, marker/cutting floor friction, ergonomic/occupational health burdens, and labor valuation in the Brazilian and global apparel industry). Therefore `synthesize` with structured phase-by-phase breakdown is optimal.

O pedido exige mapear em profundidade não apenas a lista descritiva de atribuições da modelista, mas cruzar toda a cadeia produtiva do vestuário (estilo, modelagem base/interpretação, pilotagem, prova de caimento, ficha técnica, gradação, risco/encaixe e liberação para corte/costura) com as dores técnicas, ergonômicas, organizacionais e tecnológicas documentadas na literatura acadêmica e empírica de confecção.
